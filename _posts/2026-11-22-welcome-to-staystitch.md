I was at the doctor's office with my mom when she shared that a group of her close contacts were all sharing their stories of how being a caregiver has shifted their lives in various ways.  Dr. Kahn commented that having a place online to share those stories could be helpful not only my mom, Stephanie, but could help others that may not be in her direct circle of friends and acquaintances.  From that moment I knew that Staystitch would exist.

I've had a blog for longer than I can remember at this point.  Why my mom hasn't had one until now is shameful at best.  Any words that I've thought were worthy to share with the world wide web pale in comparison to the wisdom and value that she's got to offer.  She's the smartest person I know and she has a memory that makes me question how she has room to learn something new every day and not lose something in the process.   There are many aspects of who I am, how I think and the way I have navigated the world that I owe to her.  

The irony is that while she now refers to her daily life as *being a caregiver*, she's always been one to our whole family and we're all the outcome of her life's work.  To have a place to share her observations past and present, framed in the theme of her hobby and passion, felt too fitting to pass up.

I love you Mom, and whether you decide this is for you or not, there's no obligation to post even once.  If you do, know that you'll have an engaged reader in me.
